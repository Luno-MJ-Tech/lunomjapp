How it works

Four steps from first payment to ownership.

  1. 1

    Set the contract

    The owner adds the vehicle, the price, the weekly or daily rate and the named driver. Every contract on LUNO names its driver.

  2. 2

    Pay and record

    The driver pays the way they always do and records it in the app with its reference.

    M-PesaAirtel MoneyCash
  3. 3

    Owner verifies

    The owner approves or rejects each payment, from anywhere. Both sides see the same status, and every correction leaves a trace.

  4. 4

    Own it

    When the last payment is verified, LUNO produces a completion certificate the driver can keep.

Our story

Named after a man who refused to fear.

“Luuuunooo, Luuuunooo… Luno amechoma jando.”

In 1970s southern Tanzania, Luno was a young man who had never been to school. To test the spirits everyone feared, he put a hot coal in the corner of a sacred jando hut and watched it burn. The curses never came. Then he asked his community one question:

“If these spirits are so mighty, why can they not stop the hunger, disease, and oppression we face every day?”

LUNO carries that question into the bodaboda business. Why keep trusting a notebook that keeps failing owners and drivers, when one shared record can settle every argument?

Questions

Straight answers.

Is LUNO free?

Yes. The app is currently free for owners and drivers. If we ever add paid features, we will tell you before any charge applies to you.

Why is LUNO free?

LUNO does not sell your data and shows no adverts. For now the company carries the costs so that many owners and drivers can build a payment history. Later, extra services such as SMS and SOS may be paid for; the ledger itself stays free to download.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. You can record payments with no connection. They are saved on your phone and sync when you are back online.

Which phones does LUNO work on?

Android phones with Android 8.0 or newer, from Google Play, and iPhones with iOS 16 or newer, from the App Store.

Who can see my payments?

Only the owner and the driver on the same contract. A statement or certificate leaves the app only when you share it yourself, for example with a bank, and it never shows the purchase price or profit.

Is my data safe?

Data is encrypted between the app and our servers, database rules let each person reach only their own contracts, and payments are never silently edited or deleted. We do not sell your data and the app has no advertising. LUNO is registered with Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Commission. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings and choose Delete Account, or email hello@lunomj.com from the address you sign in with. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. An owner must first close active contracts with drivers. What is removed and what is kept.

Does LUNO lend money?

No. LUNO is the record, not the lender. It does not provide finance, hold money or collect payments. The agreement is between the owner and the driver. See our Terms of Use.
