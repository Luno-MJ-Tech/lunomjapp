1. What LUNO Is, and What It Is Not

  • LUNO is a payment ledger between a vehicle owner and a driver. It records the agreement and the payments made under it, and calculates progress from what you enter. A driver can also keep a record of their own agreement without an owner on LUNO.
  • LUNO is for owners and drivers of any vehicle type, in whatever currency the owner and driver agree.
  • LUNO is not a lender. It does not provide finance, hold or move money, or collect payments.
  • LUNO is not a party to your contract. The agreement (price, tenure, rate, currency and any other terms) is between the owner and the driver alone. LUNO does not guarantee that anyone will pay, deliver a vehicle or keep their agreement, and does not settle disputes between owners and drivers.
  • Scores, rankings, reports and Kiongozi notes are calculated from the records you enter. They are information, not financial, legal or credit advice, and not a guarantee about anyone.

3. Your Records

  • You are responsible for the accuracy of everything you record: vehicles, contracts, payments, references, dates, explanations and the details of other people.

  • Payments are never silently edited or deleted. A correction or rejection always leaves a trace in the history.
  • LUNO may refuse a payment that cannot be right, for example one with a transaction reference that was already used, or one above what is left to pay on the contract.

4. No Vehicle Without a Named Driver

Every vehicle contract on LUNO must name its driver. The driver does not need to use LUNO: an owner can record the driver's name and phone number and invite the driver to join later with an invite code. LUNO does not accept idle or driverless contracts, and asks for the driver's name on any older contract saved without one.

5. Completed and Silent Contracts

  • A completed contract leaves the active dashboard but stays in your history. It is never deleted.
  • When a contract has no payment and no explanation for 30 days, the App asks you to explain ("Eleza") and we send a warning email. After 60 days it becomes dormant: hidden and read-only, with nothing deleted. A new payment, an explanation or a driver joining reopens it.

Notebook lines are saved as pending until they are verified. AI never verifies or changes a payment or a contract. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
