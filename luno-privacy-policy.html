This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why, who processes it for us, how long we keep it, and your rights under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 of Tanzania.

LUNO is a payment ledger between vehicle owners and drivers. It is for owners and drivers of any vehicle type (for example boda boda, bajaji or cars) and works in whatever currency the owner and driver agree. A driver can also keep a record of their own agreement without an owner on LUNO.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account: your name, email address, your role (owner or driver) and your app language. If you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address from them (Apple may give us a private relay address instead).
  • Vehicles and contracts: plate number, vehicle type and country, the agreed price, tenure, daily or weekly rate, currency, start and end dates, and the invite code used to connect a driver.
  • Payments: amounts, payment dates, mobile money or bank transaction references, and each payment's status (pending, verified or rejected), with any rejection reason.
  • Explanations and pauses: the reasons you record when payments stop (the "Eleza" prompt), and pauses such as a vehicle off the road.
  • Phone numbers: an owner's phone number when it is printed on a completion certificate, and phone numbers entered for drivers or owners.

2.2 Information about other people that you enter

Owners enter their driver's name and phone number, and a driver keeping their own record may enter their owner's name and phone number. The driver does not need to use LUNO. If you enter someone else's details, you must have the right to do so and let them know that their details are recorded in LUNO.

2.3 Information collected automatically

  • Notification tokens: a token for your device, its platform (Android or iPhone) and the app version, so we can send you notifications.
  • Usage and crash data (Android app): Firebase Analytics records screens viewed and a few app events (for example that a payment was recorded, with its amount and method, or the language you use). Firebase Crashlytics records crash reports with your device model and operating system. These do not include your name or phone number.

2.4 Camera and photos

  • Android: the App asks for camera permission only when you choose to scan a receipt or photograph a notebook page. Receipts are read on your phone and are never uploaded.
  • iPhone: you choose a photo with the system photo picker. The App does not ask for access to your camera or your whole photo library.
  • Notebook pages: the App first reads dates and amounts on your phone. Only if you then choose "Send the photo to the AI" and agree to the consent message ("This photo will be sent to an AI service (Google Gemini on Vertex AI) to read dates and amounts only. It may show names or phone numbers written in the notebook. LUNO does not keep the photo.") is the photo sent. Without that consent, only the dates and amounts read on your phone are sent. LUNO does not keep the photo. The lines found are saved as pending payments only after you choose them, and you verify them.

2.5 Payment SMS that you paste

You can paste a mobile money SMS to fill in a payment. The App reads it on your phone first. Only if it cannot find the amount or the reference is the text sent to our AI service, and names and phone numbers are removed on your phone before it is sent. The App does not read your SMS inbox, and LUNO does not keep the SMS text.

2.6 What we do not collect

We do not collect your location, your contacts or your SMS inbox. We never ask for or store bank account numbers, PINs or mobile money passwords.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To run the ledger: contracts, payments, verification by the owner, and the shared view between an owner and their driver.
  • To calculate progress, the payment score (amount paid divided by the amount required for the period) and the contract's ranking: highly recommended, recommended, or needs close monitoring.
  • To create statements and completion certificates when you ask for them. Statements and certificates that you can share never show the purchase price or profit; those appear only in the owner's private fleet report.
  • To send notifications you can switch off in Settings: the weekly report, daily repayment reminders ("Marejesho"), and payment updates.
  • To send emails: a welcome email, and the account and contract notices described in section 7.
  • Kiongozi, LUNO's assistant: weekly risk notes for owners, and checks that flag unusual activity, such as the same payment recorded twice. Kiongozi reads and reports only. It never changes a payment or a contract.
  • To keep LUNO secure, prevent fraud and fix problems.

4. Artificial Intelligence (Google Gemini on Vertex AI)

LUNO uses Google Gemini on Google Cloud Vertex AI for three tasks:

  • Weekly risk notes for owners: a short note explaining a contract's ranking. Gemini receives only figures and dates (amounts, days, the ranking), never names, phone numbers or plate numbers. The ranking itself is calculated by LUNO's own rules; the AI only explains it. If the AI's note breaks our rules, a fixed LUNO text is used instead.
  • Reading a pasted payment SMS that our rules could not read, with names and phone numbers removed first (section 2.5).
  • Reading a notebook page, only with your consent for the photo (section 2.4).

Google processes this data on our behalf and does not use it to train its models. LUNO does not keep the SMS text or the notebook photo. Every AI result is a suggestion: you confirm each payment, and the AI never verifies or changes a payment. Daily repayment reminders and unusual-activity checks are calculated by LUNO's own rules, not by the AI.

5. Who Processes Data for Us

We do not sell, trade or rent your personal data, and the App contains no advertising. These service providers process data on our behalf, under their data processing terms:

  • Supabase: database, sign-in and server functions. Our database is hosted in Mumbai, India.
  • Resend: sending emails.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): notifications on Android.
  • Apple Push Notification service: notifications on iPhone.
  • Google Sign-In and Sign in with Apple: signing in, if you choose them.
  • Google Gemini on Vertex AI (Google Cloud): the AI tasks in section 4.
  • Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (Google): usage and crash data from the Android app.
  • Google Analytics: visits to our website, lunomj.com (section 11).

Some of these providers process data outside Tanzania (for example in India, the United States or the European Union). We transfer data abroad only as the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 allows, to providers bound by data protection terms, and always encrypted in transit.

We may also disclose data when the law of the United Republic of Tanzania requires it.

6. Sharing Between Users

An owner and the driver connected to the same contract can both see that contract and its payments. Nobody else can see your vehicles, contracts or payments in the App. A statement or certificate is shared only when you share it yourself, for example with a bank.

7. How Long We Keep Data, and Account Lifecycle

  • While you use LUNO: we keep your data for as long as your account exists.
  • Completed contracts leave the active dashboard but are kept in your history. They are never deleted.
  • Contracts that stop recording: after 30 days with no payment and no explanation, the App asks you to explain ("Eleza") and we send a warning email. After 60 days the contract becomes dormant: it is hidden from dashboards, reports and reminders, its history becomes read-only, and nothing is deleted. A new payment, an explanation, or a driver joining reopens it.
  • Accounts with no real vehicle or contract: LUNO is for real owners and drivers. An account that has never had a vehicle, a contract or a payment gets reminders on day 7 and day 12 (email and notification) and is deleted on day 14, at least 2 days after the day-12 reminder reached you. An account that already existed when this rule started first gets a notice giving it 14 days. We never delete an account without a warning that actually reached you, and an account with any contract history is never deleted automatically. These automatic steps start on a date we announce in the App; until then, no account is deleted automatically.
  • When you delete your account (Settings → Delete Account): deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. We remove your sign-in, profile, name, phone number and notification tokens. Contracts and payments that you share with another person stay in their ledger, with your name replaced by "Former Owner" or "Former Driver". An owner must first close active contracts with drivers.
  • After deletion we keep only a count (your role and how old the account was), with no name, email or phone number. We may keep records longer where Tanzanian law requires it.

8. Security

  • All data between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit (TLS).
  • Database rules make sure each person can reach only their own data and the contracts they are part of.
  • Payments are never silently edited or deleted; every correction leaves a trace.
  • Data saved on your phone for offline use is synced securely when you are back online, and is cleared when you sign out.

9. Your Rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Act, 2022 you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is used (this policy)
  • Access a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your account and personal data, in the App (Settings → Delete Account) or by contacting us
  • Object to processing, and withdraw consent at any time, for example by switching off notifications or by not sending a notebook photo
  • Complain to the Personal Data Protection Commission (PDPC) if you believe we have not respected your rights

To use these rights, contact us at hello@lunomj.com or on WhatsApp +255 785 431 978. We may ask you to confirm your identity first.
